Installing

  1. Install rareBit from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. The watch app installs on its own when Automatic App Install is on. Otherwise: Watch app on the iPhone → Available Apps → rareBit → Install.
  3. Open rareBit on the watch. It finds your Relay on its own — no pairing screen.

Using it

  1. Tap starts and pauses the match; turn the crown on the edit screen to set the period length.
  2. Stoppage on: a tap on a running clock opens a stoppage, the next tap closes it. A triple tap every 20 s reminds you one is open.
  3. At the end of the period the watch keeps buzzing until you tap the screen.

Long press on a Flag = that Flag's haptic. Short press = one shared tap (needs a Relay with short-press support). Questions? reply@rarebit.biz
